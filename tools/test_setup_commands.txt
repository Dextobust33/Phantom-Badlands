==================================================================
  TEST SETUP COMMANDS — Gathering Minigames, Crafting, Rarity
==================================================================

After logging in with your admin account, paste these commands
into chat one group at a time. Create a new character first
(any class works, Ranger or Fighter recommended).

--- STEP 1: Level and Resources ---
/setlevel 50
/giveall

--- STEP 2: Set Gathering Jobs (needed for gathering bonuses) ---
/setjob mining 30
/setjob logging 30
/setjob foraging 30
/setjob fishing 30
/setjob soldier 50

--- STEP 3: Set Specialty Jobs (needed for crafting specialist hints) ---
/setjob blacksmith 30
/setjob alchemist 30
/setjob enchanter 30
/setjob scribe 30

--- STEP 4: Give Gathering Tools (equip from Inventory > Tools) ---
/givetool pickaxe 3
/givetool axe 3
/givetool sickle 3
/givetool rod 3

--- STEP 5: Give Crafting Materials (for blacksmithing + alchemy) ---
/givemats copper_ore 99

==================================================================
  TESTING CHECKLIST
==================================================================

GATHERING MINIGAMES:
  [ ] Mining: Walk to ore deposit (mountains), press R to Mine
      → Check: Depth meter shows. At depth 3+, "DEEP VEIN" bonus
  [ ] Fishing: Walk to water (~), press R to Fish
      → Check: Size roll (Small/Medium/Large/Trophy) shown
  [ ] Logging: Walk to dense forest, press R to Chop
      → Check: Momentum bar. At 3/5/7 correct, "BONUS CHOP"
  [ ] Foraging: Walk to grassland, press R to Forage
      → Check: Plant names shown (not generic). "NEW DISCOVERY"
        on first correct. Known plants marked green ◆

SOLDIER HARVEST:
  [ ] Kill a monster (walk into one)
      → Check: Harvest prompt with mastery level + saves shown
      → Wrong answer should give "save" message (soldier Lv50 = 2 saves)

CRAFTING MINIGAME:
  [ ] Go to Trading Post, select Crafting
      → Pick Blacksmithing, craft a weapon recipe
      → Check: 3-round challenge with skill-themed questions
      → Specialist hint "[Risky]" shown on one wrong answer
      → Score (0-3) shown in result
  [ ] Try auto-skip: craft a very low difficulty recipe
      → Should skip minigame (when skill - difficulty >= 30)

RARITY EFFECTS:
  [ ] Craft several items, inspect them (Inventory > select item)
      → Uncommon+ should show rarity bonuses (ATK%, crit, etc.)
  [ ] Equip crafted weapon → fight monster → verify crit bonuses
  [ ] Equip crafted armor → fight monster → verify dodge/DR work

CONSUMABLE MULTI-USE:
  [ ] Craft epic/legendary potion → verify "Uses: 2" or "Uses: 3"
  [ ] Use it once → still in inventory. Use again → consumed.
