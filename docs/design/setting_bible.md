# Phantom Badlands — Setting Bible

**Status:** agreed 2026-09-01 (backlog item 3). This is the reference the Dungeon Atlas, quest
text, NPC flavour, egg/companion text and the website are all written against. Keep it to a
page. If something here stops being true in the game, change this file first, then the copy.

---

## The premise, in one line

**The Badlands is ground that does not let go of what it takes — and it takes places as
readily as it takes people.**

## What a Phantom is

A phantom is something the land has kept and pushed back up.

It keeps **places**: somewhere that existed once surfaces again, whole enough to walk into.
Those are the dungeons.

It keeps **people**: the delvers who go into those places don't come back all the way. They
last a while, then they don't. Those are the player characters.

The two are the same phenomenon, and that is the whole conceit: **going into a phantom place
is what makes you one.** Every delve leaves a little more of you behind. Permadeath is not bad
luck — it's the bill. Everyone in the Badlands knows delvers are short-lived; that's why the
posts trade with them so freely and don't ask them to stay.

The one thing that moves cleanly between lives is what you brought out **alive**.

## The Badlands

A frontier that lost. Not a wasteland of nothing — a wasteland of *too much*, ground so
crowded with what it won't release that nowhere stays settled.

The **trading posts are holdouts, not cities**. Each one keeps a patch of land thin and quiet
around itself; that patch is the only reliable safety there is. Walk away from a post and the
land thickens — which is exactly the post-anchored level curve the game already runs. Distance
from a post *is* danger, and everyone living there knows it in those terms.

Posts hold because they are useful to delvers and delvers are useful to them. It's a working
arrangement between people who need each other, not a heroic alliance.

## Dungeons — phantom places

Dungeons **surface**. They are not built, discovered, or abandoned; the land pushes them up.
A phantom place takes root, and then it starts **bleeding** — monsters work outward from it
toward the nearest holdout. That is the threat corridor, and it is the reason anyone delves:
not treasure, containment.

Clearing one buys a real reprieve. It does not buy a permanent one. The land is still holding
whatever it was holding, and another will surface. Posts measure their safety in seasons.

A dungeon's **theme is what it kept** — a den, a crypt, a cathedral, a hatchery. The monsters
inside are not squatters; they are part of what surfaced.

## Delvers — phantom people

You are temporary and the world treats you accordingly. You are not the chosen anything. You
are one of many, you are useful, and you will be replaced.

- **Death is permanent** because you were always partly claimed.
- **Your corpse stays where you fell.** Somebody else will find it. So will you, next life.
- **The leaderboard, titles and Valor are the trace you leave** — the Badlands' way of
  remembering a name after the person is gone.

Write delvers with dignity, not tragedy. They know the terms. The tone is working people doing
dangerous work, not doomed heroes.

## Companions — what comes out alive

**Eggs come out of phantom places.** They are the one living thing a dead place produces, and
that is the point: a companion at your side is proof you went in and came out.

Companions are the setting's emotional spine because **they outlive their delvers**. When you
die, your companion doesn't. The Sanctuary keeps it, and it goes on with whoever comes next.
The bond is the only thing in the Badlands with continuous memory — you are the part that gets
replaced.

- **Variants and border tier** = how much of the place it came from it still carries.
- **Fusion** is not crafting. It is letting several become something older and less divided.
- **Breeding and the kennel** are how a line persists past any one of your lives.

## The Sanctuary — the keeping-place

The Sanctuary is yours across every life: the one address in the Badlands that stays. It holds
what survives you — companions above all. It is not a home you live in; it's the place your
things wait while you are being replaced.

## Voice — how to write anything in this world

1. **Plain, worked, unromantic.** Posts talk like trade towns on a bad border. No high fantasy
   register, no prophecy, no capital-D Destiny.
2. **Containment over glory.** Quests are jobs: drive it off, clear it, get them out, bring it
   back. Nobody is saving the world; they are keeping this month survivable.
3. **The land is the antagonist, not a villain.** Nothing is malevolent. It simply doesn't let
   go. Avoid a dark lord.
4. **Delver mortality is stated flatly, never mourned.** "Most don't last two seasons." Never
   "tragically fallen."
5. **Companions are the warmth.** If a line of text is going to have feeling in it, that is
   where it belongs.
6. **Never explain the mechanism.** Nobody in the Badlands knows *why* the land keeps things.
   Theories exist; none are right. Resist a canonical answer — the mystery is load-bearing.

**Use:** surfaced, took root, bleeding, holdout, thin ground, delver, kept, came out alive,
reprieve, seasons.
**Avoid:** ancient evil, chosen one, sealed away, the darkness, corruption, prophecy, realm.

## What this already explains

Every line here is a mechanic the game runs today. That's the test — the fiction is a reading
of the game, not a coat of paint on it.

| Mechanic already built | Now means |
|---|---|
| Dungeons spawn on a world timer | The land keeps pushing places back up |
| Threat cones aimed at posts | A surfaced place bleeds toward the nearest holdout |
| Post-anchored monster levels | Posts keep the ground thin around them |
| Post threat cooldown after clearing | A reprieve, measured in seasons |
| Eggs / exclusive cards only from dungeons | The only things that come out alive |
| Permadeath | The bill for going in |
| Sanctuary survives death | The one address that stays |
| Kennel companions passing between characters | The bond outlives the delver |
| Corpses left in the world | Delvers are replaced, and the land keeps the evidence |
| Leaderboard / titles / Valor | How a name is remembered after the person |
| Fusion / Tier Ascension | Letting several become something older |
| Border tier on companions | How much of its origin it still carries |

## Deliberately undecided

- **Why** the land keeps things. Never answer this in copy (see voice rule 6).
- Whether phantom places are echoes of somewhere *real* — leave ambiguous.
- Whether anyone has ever stopped being a delver and lived. Rumour only.
