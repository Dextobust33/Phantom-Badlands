# 👻 Welcome to Phantom Badlands

A text-based multiplayer RPG where every death is permanent. Form parties, delve dungeons, craft legendary gear, and build a legacy — one life at a time.

## 🎮 Get Started
1. **Download:** https://phantombadlands.com/download.html
2. Extract and run `PhantomBadlandsLauncher.exe` — it auto-updates the client
3. Create an account and your first character
4. Type `/help` in-game for a full guide

## 📜 Rules

**1. Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.

**2. Keep channels on-topic.** Use #off-topic for non-game chat. Use #bugs for bugs, #suggestions for ideas, #help for questions.

**3. No spam.** No link/invite spam, no excessive pings, no all-caps walls, no self-promo without permission.

**4. No NSFW content.** This is a general-audience game.

**5. No cheating, exploits, or account sharing.** Report exploits in #bugs privately — do not share them publicly.

**6. Use English in public channels** so everyone can follow along.

**7. One account per person on Discord.** Alt accounts used to evade bans will be banned.

**8. Follow Discord's Terms of Service:** https://discord.com/terms
